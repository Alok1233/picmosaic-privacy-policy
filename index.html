Official Privacy Policy

Developer: The Particles  |  Package: com.theparticles.picmosaic
Effective Date: October 13, 2026 • Version 1.0

Privacy Summary: PicMosaic is an offline-first photo framing, collage, and typography application. We never collect, upload, store, or sell your personal photos, camera images, or edited artwork. All photo processing and image composition happen 100% locally on your device.

1. Overview & Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how The Particles ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you install and use our Android mobile application PicMosaic: Grid, Frames & Art (the "Application" or "App"), available on Google Play.

We respect your privacy. This policy describes our practices regarding the processing of photos, device storage, third-party advertising, and network communications.

2. Photo & Media Access (Zero-Permission Architecture)

PicMosaic complies with Google Play's strict Photo and Video Permissions Policy:

  • Privacy FirstNo Broad Storage Permissions: The App does not request or require dangerous storage permissions such as READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE or unconstrained READ_MEDIA_IMAGES.
  • System Photo PickerAndroid System Photo Picker: When you select pictures for framing or collages, the App invokes the official Android System Photo Picker (ActivityResultContracts.PickMultipleVisualMedia). You explicitly grant access only to the exact photos you choose.
  • Local Processing100% On-Device Rendering: Selected photos are decoded and composited strictly inside your device's local memory (RAM). Your photos are never transmitted to our servers, cloud backends, or third parties.
  • Scoped StorageSaving Creations: Exported artwork is saved directly to your device's public Pictures directory (Pictures/Glitter Frames or Pictures/PicMosaic) using Android Scoped Storage (MediaStore API).

3. Information We Collect

A. Personal Data

We do not collect personal identifying information. We do not require registration, accounts, names, email addresses, phone numbers, or social logins to use the App.

B. Cloud Frame Manifest & Asset Downloads

The Application connects to our Content Delivery Network (AWS CloudFront / Amazon S3) solely to fetch updated decorative frame manifests (manifest.json) and download high-resolution frame overlays on demand:

  • Standard HTTP requests transmit standard network headers (e.g., User-Agent, IP address as required for HTTP routing).
  • No user identifiers or personal photo data are sent during these asset requests.
  • Downloaded frame files are cached locally in app-private storage so you can use them offline.

C. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Application displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob to support ongoing development and maintenance. Google AdMob may collect and process certain device information in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy:

  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID / GAID) for ad personalization (where permitted by user settings).
  • Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and carrier.
  • Ad interaction and performance metrics (impressions, clicks, error logs).

Our advertising implementation complies with Google Play Better Ads policies: ads are shown strictly at natural transition breaks (such as after completing an image export) and never during creative canvas editing or on app back-press.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA / GDPR-K)

Our Application is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take immediate corrective measures.

6. Data Security

Because PicMosaic does not operate user accounts or cloud storage of user photos, your personal media remains exclusively under your physical device's security controls. All network communications for decorative frame templates utilize industry-standard HTTPS encryption (TLS 1.2+).

7. Your Rights & Ad Choices

You can manage how advertising identifiers are shared directly in your Android device settings:

  • Reset Advertising ID: Go to Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID.
  • Delete Advertising ID: Go to Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID to opt out of personalized ads completely.
